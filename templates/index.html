Gas Price Calculation

The gas price per MWh is calculated as follows:

  1. Base MBTU per MWh: 3.412
  2. Adjustment for calorific value: Divide by (Gas kCal / 8500)
  3. Calculate gas price per MWh: (Gas Price per MBTU × Base MBTU per MWh × Adjustment) / (Generator Efficiency / 100)

Higher kCal values result in lower costs since more energy is available per unit of gas.
